MATLAB Verify

Verify AI math in active or passive mode.

A browser extension sends math and engineering claims to a local MATLAB listener, then returns a verdict in the chat workflow.

Active verification is always available for highlighted text. Passive mode is an optional beta that watches ChatGPT answers and annotates likely claims automatically.

Two workflows

Manual when you want control, automatic when you want coverage.

The combined extension keeps active verification always on and makes passive checking an explicit beta toggle.

1

Browser extension

Captures highlighted text for active verification or candidate response blocks for passive verification.

2

MATLAB listener

Receives local requests on localhost and routes claims to the right MATLAB verifier.

3

Verdict in context

Returns verified, not verified, or insufficient-context results directly in the page.
